# Death Valley — November 2021

Two days in the park, November 20–21, 2021. A vehicle-based route through the main corridors: Dante's View, Zabriskie Point, Golden Canyon, Artist's Palette, Badwater Basin, Natural Bridge Canyon, Ubehebe Crater, Harmony Borax Works, Salt Creek, and the Rhyolite ghost town on the approach.

248 frames. Landscapes and geology carry the series — eroded Furnace Creek Formation lakebed sediments, salt crusts at the basin floor, slot canyon narrows, a natural arch, abandoned mine workings, and the borax works at Furnace Creek.

## Salt Creek

The quiet center of the series. A relict desert-spring ecosystem photographed as a small survey: pupfish (*Cyprinodon* sp.) holding in shallow water, water striders (Gerridae) on the surface film, pickleweed (*Salicornia*) along the banks, cyanobacterial mats in the shallows. Fish descended from a lake system that dried roughly ten thousand years ago, persisting in the driest basin in North America.

## Species recorded

- Water strider — Gerridae
